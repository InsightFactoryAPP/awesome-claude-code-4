## What Is a Model Multiplier?

The model multiplier determines how much of your weekly quota is consumed when using a given model family.

Formula:

```text
Official model cost × model multiplier = weekly quota consumed
```

A lower multiplier means your quota lasts longer; a higher multiplier means faster consumption.

For the Max plan (210 USD/week), if you use only OpenAI Codex or Google Gemini:

```text
210 ÷ 0.6 = 350
```

You can use up to approximately **350 USD** of official Codex / Gemini quota per week.

If you use only Anthropic Claude:

```text
210 ÷ 1 = 210
```

You can use up to approximately **210 USD** of official Claude quota per week.

If you mix models, the system calculates each separately and sums them. For example, 100 USD of Codex and 50 USD of Claude:

```text
100 × 0.6 + 50 × 1 = 110
```

This deducts 110 USD from the 210 USD weekly quota.

## What Is the Effective Exchange Rate?

When your plan quota is fully used, the effective exchange rate shows how many CNY you pay per 1 USD of official model quota consumed.

For the Max plan:

| Model Family | Effective Rate | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| OpenAI Codex / Google Gemini | 0.53 CNY/USD | 1 USD of Codex/Gemini costs ~0.53 CNY |
| Anthropic Claude | 0.88 CNY/USD | 1 USD of Claude costs ~0.88 CNY |

<Callout type="info">
The effective exchange rate is not the live USD/CNY forex rate. It is a derived cost reference calculated from plan price, weekly quota, and model multiplier.
</Callout>

## Multiplier Adjustment Notice

<Callout type="warning">
Model multipliers and effective exchange rates may be adjusted as market conditions change. Users will be notified at least 3 days in advance of any adjustment.
</Callout>

## What Is the Session Window Limit?

The session window limit caps how much quota can be consumed within a fixed time window. For example, the Max plan's 35 USD / 5-hour limit means at most 35 USD of weekly quota can be consumed in any 5-hour period.

- **Window limit**: controls short-term usage intensity
- **Weekly quota**: controls total usage over a week

## What Is the Weekly Quota?

The weekly quota is the total quota available per week.

## FAQ

### Does a lower multiplier mean better value?

Yes. A lower multiplier means the same 1 USD of official model usage consumes less weekly quota. For example, 0.6× means 1 USD of model quota only consumes 0.6 USD of weekly quota.

### Is the effective exchange rate the same as the forex rate?

No. It is a derived cost reference showing how much CNY you pay per 1 USD of official quota when your plan is fully utilized.

### How is quota calculated when mixing models?

The system calculates each model's consumption separately and sums the total.

### Will multipliers change?

Possibly. Users will be notified at least 3 days before any adjustment.
